Wireless charging

Texas Guadaloop · In progress

Wireless charging and battery backup for Texas Guadaloop’s Gen V pod. In development, targeting 250 kHz operation across an 8-inch air gap.

High-frequency current in the transmitter coil creates a changing magnetic field that transfers power to the pod’s receiver coil. The system combines wireless power with battery backup to keep levitation powered.
